## Thesis
For music lovers aged 20 to 40 craving authentic community and belonging, illieisalover delivers Somatic House, DJ performances and synced audio/visual sets that heal the nervous system through music, because Illie leads with authentic relating and safety rather than just a set.

## Offer
Music, community, a sense of belonging, and DJ performances with synced audio/visuals, built around a brand of house music Illie calls "Somatic House." Priced at $300 per DJ gig. Solves the customer's want for a real sense of community and belonging from the artists they follow, and for nervous system healing through music. Why Illie rather than the obvious alternative: he leads with authentic relating and safety, against a scene he sees as too often driven by ego, status and money.
