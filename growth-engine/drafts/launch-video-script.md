# Launch video script, redraft

Written after reviewing Illie's original launch video (visual only, plus the transcript he supplied) and rewriting the words in his captured voice.

**Hook (spoken):** The first time this happened to me, I wasn't even the one playing.

**Script:** I was just on the floor, and something happened in my body like those cymatics videos, where sound gets pumped into water and it holds these perfect geometric shapes. I became the water droplet. I moved through shape after shape I didn't choose, and by the end of it, something that had been stuck in me for fifteen years finally let go.

That's the whole reason I'm building this. It's called illieisalover, and the sound is Somatic House, music built to move what's stuck in your body, not just get you dancing. We're going to use the dance floor the way it was always meant to be used: to heal, to actually feel safe next to strangers, to find real community instead of a scene built on ego.

So come find me on the floor. I can't wait to give you what that night gave me.
